# discover

The human decides; the agent prepares, synthesises and challenges.

1. Read `docs/product/brief.md` and `docs/product/assumptions.md`.
2. If the brief is empty, interview the human: the job without a solution, the user groups, goals and non-goals, the value metric and its inputs. Write the brief from the answers.
3. Update the opportunity tree from the interview log. Each opportunity must come from a specific past episode a real user told; flag any that come only from the builders.
4. List assumptions across value, usability, feasibility and viability. Propose the riskiest one and the cheapest test for it, with 3–5 outside users per distinct group.
5. Allowed AI help: desk research (verify each claim at its source), drafting and piloting the interview guide, transcription, first-pass coding of notes. Never simulate users or treat model answers as evidence.
6. Propose the walking skeleton: the thinnest path across every main activity.
7. Stop. The human picks the assumption to test and approves the MVP slice. Record the decision in `assumptions.md`.
